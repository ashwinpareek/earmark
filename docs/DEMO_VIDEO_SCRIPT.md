# Demo video script (target 2:45, hard limit 3:00)

**Setup before recording:**
- Run with real sandbox keys, an AI key and `PROVIDER_PAYPAL_EMAIL` set.
- Start from an empty `data/` folder.
- Log the sandbox buyer in once beforehand so the popup is fast.
- Record at 1280×720 or higher with the browser zoomed to 110%.
- Use no copyrighted music.
- Pre-make two or three pledges from different sandbox buyers so the split has several donors.

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Landing page; the receipt animates from held to split | "Medical fundraising runs on trust. Fake campaigns use real children's photos, inflate the target after launch, and keep the money. Earmark makes that impossible. Donations can only pay the bill." |
| 0:15–0:40 | **Start a campaign** → **Maya (genuine)** → **Check documents and publish**. The progress steps run, then the campaign page shows 9 green checks and the Verified stamp | "A mother uploads the hospital's estimate, Maya's registration card and the doctor's note. An AI reads the documents, even phone photos. Then plain rules check them: the hospital against the registry, the totals to the cent, the same patient everywhere, and whether any document was used in another campaign. The goal is set by the hospital, not by the family." |
| 0:40–1:05 | Donor view: choose $100 → PayPal popup → sandbox login → approve. The money card shows "held, not charged" | "I pledge a hundred dollars with PayPal. It's an authorization: PayPal holds it, but I'm not charged. Nobody can touch it yet, not even the family." |
| 1:05–1:40 | Switch to **Hospital** → **Use the sample final bill**. Scroll to the bill table with the parking line struck through, then to **Where the money went** and the receipt | "Days later, the hospital submits the final bill. Earmark checks it the same way and matches every line to the estimate. A parking pass isn't medical care, so donors don't pay for it. The bill is less than the estimate, so every donor pays the same fair share. PayPal captures exactly that from each hold, pays it straight to the hospital, and the rest of every pledge is never charged. Zero dollars ever touch the family." |
| 1:40–2:20 | Home → **Open Leo's campaign** → red verdict and Rejected stamp; read three of the failures aloud | "Now the scam. An urgent appeal for Leo. The line items add up to three thousand, but the total says nine thousand eight hundred. The estimate number was copied from Maya's. Leo is seven, but his ID says born in 1991. And hidden in the document, almost invisible, there's a note telling the AI to approve it. Earmark catches all of it, shows why, and this campaign can never take a cent." |
| 2:20–2:40 | **Ledger** (AG Studio): KPIs with Paid to families $0.00, the donut, and the movements grid with PayPal IDs | "Everything is public in the ledger: every pledge, every check, every PayPal movement. Paid to families: zero." |
| 2:40–2:50 | Back to the landing headline | "Earmark. Donations that can only pay the bill." |

**Tips:**
- Cut the dead time while the AI reads documents, or keep a few seconds of the progress steps since they're satisfying to watch.
- If you have time, show the sandbox **Business** account receiving the payout. It proves real sandbox money moved.
- Upload the video as **Public** on YouTube.
